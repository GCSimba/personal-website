第一性原理不是寻找答案，而是重新定义问题

First Principles Is Not About Finding an Answer—It Is About Reframing the Question

第一性原理最有价值的地方，不是把复杂问题拆成更小的零件，而是先把问题从默认叙事里拿出来，重新判断“真正要解决的是什么”。当问题被重新定义，答案空间也会随之改变。

The real value of first-principles thinking is not simply breaking a complex problem into smaller parts. It is taking the problem out of its default narrative and asking what actually needs to be solved. Once the question is reframed, the space of possible answers changes with it.
